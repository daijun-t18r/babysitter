# Knowledge: Development (reassurance framing)

Canonical facts:
- Milestones are RANGES, wide ones: social smile 6-12 weeks; rolling 3-6 months; sitting unsupported 4-9 months; crawling 6-12 months (some healthy babies skip it entirely); first words around 12 months; walking 9-18 months.
- Preterm babies: always judge milestones by CORRECTED age until age 2.
- Normal-but-worrying-at-3am: newborn startle (Moro) reflex, occasional crossed eyes before 4 months, hiccups, irregular "periodic" breathing in short bursts DURING SLEEP (brief speed-ups and 5-10 second pauses without color change), quivering chin, sleep twitches.
- Default framing: reassure with the range, name what's on track, and note that the pediatrician reviews development at every well visit — 3am is rarely the moment a milestone question must be settled.

Try this first:
- 0-3m: tummy time in small daily doses builds everything later; babies fed and gaining who track faces are doing their one job.
- 3-6m: rolling changes sleep and swaddle rules; wobbly sitting practice with support.
- 6-12m: separation anxiety is a milestone, not a setback; babbling variety matters more than clear words.

Red flags (mention gently, route to pediatrician rather than emergency unless acute): no social smile by 3 months, not responding to sounds, persistent stiffness or marked floppiness, loss of previously-had skills, no babbling by 9-10 months. Acute floppiness or unresponsiveness TONIGHT is an emergency, not a development question.
