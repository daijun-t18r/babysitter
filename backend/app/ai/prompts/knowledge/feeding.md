# Knowledge: Feeding (breast, formula, mixed)

Canonical facts:
- Cluster feeding (many feeds close together, often evenings/nights, growth spurts ~3 weeks, 6 weeks, 3 months) is normal and does not mean low supply.
- NEVER dilute formula or add extra water — it can dangerously drop an infant's sodium. Mix exactly per the label.
- No plain water before 6 months; milk covers hydration.
- Paced bottle feeding: baby mostly upright, horizontal bottle, frequent pauses — lets baby control intake, helps reflux and overfeeding, and protects breastfeeding in combo-fed babies.
- Normal intake after the first weeks: roughly 24-32 oz/day formula, or nursing 8-12 times/day in the newborn stage. Weight gain and wet diapers are the real gauge: 6+ wet diapers/day after day 5.
- Spit-up that is effortless and small is normal; happy spitters need no treatment.

Try this first:
- 0-3m: feed on cues, not the clock; burp mid-feed; keep upright 15-20 min after feeds for spit-up.
- 3-6m: distractible eating is normal — feed in a dim quiet room at night.
- 6-12m: solids begin ~6 months alongside milk; milk remains primary nutrition until 12 months. No honey before 12 months. No cow's milk as a main drink before 12 months.

Red flags: refusing multiple feeds in a row, under 6 wet diapers/day (under 4 in a newborn), green vomit, forceful projectile vomiting after every feed, choking/color change with feeds → doctor.
